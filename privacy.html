Privacy Policy

Astrophobia Uploader · last updated 19 October 2026

Who uses it

Astrophobia Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel Astrophobia, signed in with the owner's own Google account. It has no other users and no sign-up.

What it accesses

  • YouTube upload: to publish the channel's own videos, subtitles and playlists.
  • YouTube comments (youtube.force-ssl): to read comments on the channel's own videos and post the channel's replies.
  • YouTube Analytics (read-only): to read the audience-retention curves of the channel's own videos.

How data is handled

  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer and is never shared.
  • Comment text is processed to draft replies and is not sold, shared or kept beyond the channel's own working files.
  • No data is collected from viewers beyond what YouTube shows the channel owner, and nothing is used for advertising or passed to third parties for their own use.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. YouTube's own terms and Google's privacy policy also apply: YouTube Terms of Service, Google Privacy Policy.
